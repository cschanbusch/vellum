# The Vellum name

"Vellum Game Launcher", "Vellum" (as the name of this launcher) and its logo and icon identify the
original project by Charles Schanbusch. The software licences in this repository cover code and
art, not the name. This policy says how you may use the name.

## Forks and modified versions

You may call a fork or modified version "Vellum" **with a qualifier** that makes it clear it is
not the original, for example "Vellum Couch Edition", "Vellum (Sam's fork)" or "Vellum Redux", as
long as all of the following hold:

- It says, in its README and its About screen, that it is an unofficial fork, not made or
  endorsed by Charles Schanbusch, and links to the original project.
- It does not use the original logo or icon unchanged.
- Nothing about it could reasonably make people think it is the original, an official edition or
  its successor.

Not allowed without permission:

- the bare names "Vellum" or "Vellum Game Launcher" for anything other than the original;
- names that suggest an official or newer version, such as "Vellum 2", "Vellum Pro", "Official
  Vellum" or "Vellum Plus";
- saying or implying that Charles Schanbusch made, endorses or supports it.

## Plugins

Plugins may say what they work with, such as "a skin for Vellum" or "Vellum provider for itch.io",
without permission, as long as they don't suggest they are official.

## Asking

If you're unsure, or want to use the name another way, ask Charles Schanbusch:
charlieschanbusch@gmail.com, or @orcinusprime on Discord.
