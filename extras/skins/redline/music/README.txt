Grand Tour music

Put MIDI files here, named after the view they play in:

  garage.mid      the Garage (showroom)
  covers.mid      Covers
  spotlight.mid   Spotlight
  list.mid        List

A view with no file is silent. Switching views changes the music on the next bar line of
the track that is playing, and the new track picks up at the same point: the same beat for
tracks of the same length (write them as variations of one piece), else the same share of
the way through.

The instruments come from the SoundFont you put in this folder (the first .sf2, .sf3 or .dls
file). Without one, nothing plays.

The music waits for the PRESS ANY BUTTON title screen and follows Settings > General > Sound.
