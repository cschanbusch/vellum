Grand Tour music

Put MIDI files here, named after the view they play in:

  garage.mid      the Garage (showroom)
  covers.mid      Covers
  spotlight.mid   Spotlight
  list.mid        List

A view with no file is silent. Switching views changes the music on the next beat of the
track that is playing, and the new track picks up at the same point: the same beat for
tracks of the same length (write them as variations of one piece), else the same share of
the way through.

Grand Tour ships with a track for each of these views and GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian
Collins as the instruments (see LICENSE.txt). Replace any of them with your own: the instruments
come from the first .sf2, .sf3 or .dls file in this folder.

The music waits for the PRESS ANY BUTTON title screen and follows Settings > General > Sound.
