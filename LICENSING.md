Vellum Game Launcher is made of several parts under different licences. Each folder with its own
`LICENSE` (or `LICENSE.md`) file is governed by that file; everything else in this repository is
the core, under the GPL.

| Part | Where | Licence | In short |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vellum Game Launcher** (the core) | `src-tauri/`, `shell/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `docs/` and everything else without its own licence | [GPL-3.0-only](LICENSE) with a [plugin exception](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md) | Free to use, change and share; changes you share must stay open under the GPL. Plugins may use any licence, including paid. Closed or paid redistribution of Vellum itself needs a commercial licence. |
| **SDK** | `sdk/` | [Apache-2.0](sdk/LICENSE) | Free to use for anything; keep the [NOTICE](sdk/NOTICE) attribution. |
| **Default skins**: Basis, Synthwave, Gridline, Frutiger Aero | `plugins/skins/basis/`, `extras/skins/{synthwave,gridline,aero}/` | Apache-2.0 | Free to use for anything, including as the start of your own skin; keep the NOTICE: "Based on the default skins of Vellum Game Launcher". |
| **Providers**: Steam, Epic, GOG, Custom, Mock | `plugins/providers/*/`, `extras/providers/mock/` | [MIT-0](plugins/providers/steam/LICENSE) | Completely free: no conditions, not even attribution. |
| **Modules**: Quick Launch, Random Pick | `extras/modules/*/` | MIT-0 | Completely free. |
| **Showcase skins**: FICSIT Terminal, Calling Card, Frontier, Grand Tour | `extras/skins/{ficsit,calling-card,frontier,redline}/` | [PolyForm Strict 1.0.0](extras/skins/redline/LICENSE.md), plus a permission for personal changes | Source-available: use and learn from them, change them for yourself, but don't redistribute them or works based on them without permission. |

Third-party parts keep their own licences: fonts under the SIL Open Font License (an `-OFL.txt`
beside each), three.js under MIT (`extras/skins/redline/lib/three-LICENSE.txt`), and the libraries
compiled into the exe, listed in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` in each release (generated by
`node scripts/third-party-notices.mjs`).

Plugins (providers, skins, modules) are separate works. You may license and sell them however you
like; see the [plugin exception](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md). If you start from the SDK or a default
skin, keep its NOTICE.
